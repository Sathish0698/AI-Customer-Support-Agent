# Store Policies

## Returns
You can return most items within 10 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and have all tags attached. Innerwear, personalised items and gift cards cannot be returned. To start a return, go to My Orders in the app and choose Return Item.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method. UPI and card refunds take 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned item. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or store credit, which the customer chooses. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item was damaged or wrong.

## Cancellations
An order can be cancelled for free until it is shipped. Once it has shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but the item can be returned after delivery under the return policy. If a prepaid order is cancelled, the refund takes 5 to 7 business days.

## Shipping and delivery
Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days. Orders above 499 rupees ship free; below that, shipping costs 49 rupees. We ship across India through BlueDart, Delhivery and Ecom Express. Tracking numbers are available once an order has shipped.

## Delayed orders
If an order is more than 3 days past its expected delivery date, the customer can ask for the order to be escalated to our shipping team. We cannot promise a new delivery date until the carrier confirms it.

## Warranty
Electronics such as earbuds and speakers have a 1 year manufacturer warranty against defects. Warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage or misuse. Warranty claims need the order ID and a short description of the problem.

## Payment
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 5000 rupees. If a payment fails but money was deducted, it is automatically refunded within 5 to 7 business days.

## Contact and support hours
Customer support is available from 9 AM to 9 PM IST, Monday to Saturday. For complaints that the assistant cannot resolve, a human agent can follow up by email within 24 hours.
